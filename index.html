Find My Music

Snap a CD or record. Play it on Spotify or Apple Music.

Front cover, back cover or barcode: any of them works.

The app reads the name printed on the cover, on your device. The first photo downloads a 275 MB reader, once. For covers with no text, .

Settings

With a key, your photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude to recognise the cover, even with no text on it. Without one, the app scans barcodes and reads the text on the cover with a small model that runs on your device (a one-time 275 MB download). The key is stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic. Get one at console.anthropic.com.
